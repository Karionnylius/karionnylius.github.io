Draksyn Reels Publisher

Phonk music reels → YouTube automation

Draksyn Reels Publisher is a private tool operated by Karim, the artist behind draksyn. It turns draksyn's own phonk tracks into short, beat-synced vertical music reels and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel using the official YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Analyses a draksyn track (tempo, build-up, drops) and cuts publicly released game and anime trailer footage to its beat, with the footage source credited in every video description.
  • Ends every reel with the song's cover art, title and the artist name.
  • Uploads the finished video to the operator's own channel on a schedule, sets its title, description, tags and thumbnail, and records the resulting video id.

Google account access

The app requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes for a single account: the one that owns the draksyn channel. They are used only to upload the operator's own reels, set their thumbnails, and read the upload status and view counts of those same videos. The app never reads viewer data, comments, messages or audience information, and it acts for no one else's account.

Who it is for

Nobody signs in to this app but the operator. There are no other users, no accounts to create and no data collected from visitors to this page.
