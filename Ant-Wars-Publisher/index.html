Ant-Wars Publisher

Colony war simulator → YouTube automation

Ant-Wars Publisher is a private tool operated by Karim. It simulates wars between ant colonies, renders the matches as video, and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel using the official YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Runs a deterministic ant-colony simulation: up to eight colonies fight over one map.
  • Renders each match to MP4, cuts short vertical clips from the best moments, and adds music the operator owns the rights to.
  • Uploads those files to the operator's own channel, sets the title, description and thumbnail, and records the resulting video id.

Google account access

The app requests the youtube.upload and youtube scopes for a single account: the one that owns the Ant-Wars channel. Those scopes are used only to upload the operator's own generated videos and to set their thumbnails. The app never reads viewer data, comments, messages or audience information, and it acts for no one else's account.

Who it is for

Nobody signs in to this app but the operator. There are no other users, no accounts to create and no data collected from visitors to this page.
