VIVO Volt Publisher

Original music videos → YouTube automation

VIVO Volt Publisher is a private tool operated by Karim for the VIVO Volt music channel. It turns the operator's own original instrumental music into long-form music videos and uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel using the official YouTube Data API.

What the app does

  • Masters the operator's own tracks and renders a video for each one, and for compilations of them, with visuals generated entirely by the app from the music itself: no third-party footage.
  • Uploads one finished video per day to the operator's own channel, sets its title, description, chapters, tags and thumbnail, and records the resulting video id.
  • Reads back the processing status of those uploads so the same video is never uploaded twice.

Google account access

The app requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes for a single account: the one that owns the VIVO Volt channel. They are used only to upload the operator's own videos, set their thumbnails, and read the status of those same videos. The app never reads viewer data, comments, messages or audience information, and it acts for no one else's account.

Who it is for

Nobody signs in to this app but the operator. There are no other users, no accounts to create and no data collected from visitors to this page.
