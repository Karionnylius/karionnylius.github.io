Privacy Policy

AnimeXAuraXEdits Publisher · last updated 29 September 2026

AnimeXAuraXEdits Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by Karim. It generates short anime edits set to the operator's own released music and publishes them to the ANIMEXAURAXEDITS YouTube channel, which the operator owns and controls. The app has no other users.

What the app does

The app renders video files on the operator's own computer and, on a schedule the operator sets, uploads them to the operator's YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API v3.

Data the app accesses

  • An OAuth token for the operator's own YouTube account, used solely to upload videos the app generated, set their thumbnails and read the status of those uploads. The token is stored locally on the operator's own computer and is never shared or transmitted anywhere else.
  • Video files, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails created by the app itself.
  • The video ids, privacy status, processing state and public view / like / comment counts of the operator's own uploads, returned by YouTube. They are kept in a local log so the same video is never uploaded twice.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data belonging to any other person. It does not read subscribers, comments, messages, watch history, analytics or audience data. It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training models. Visitors to this website are not tracked; there are no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Scopes requested

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload the operator's edits and set their thumbnails.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — confirm the correct channel is linked and read the status and public counts of the operator's own uploads.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Rendered videos and the small upload log (file name, video id, title, upload time) are kept on the operator's computer and can be deleted by the operator at any time. The OAuth token stays valid until the operator revokes it at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the app's access; the stored token and upload log are then deleted. Uploaded videos can be deleted by the operator in YouTube Studio at any time.

Third parties

The only third party that receives data is Google/YouTube, which receives the uploaded videos and their metadata. Nothing is shared with anyone else.
