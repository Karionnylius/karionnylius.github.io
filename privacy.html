Privacy Policy — Chess Reels Publisher

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Chess Reels Publisher is a private tool operated by Karim Nafili that renders short chess videos and publishes them to social media accounts that we own and control.

What the app does

The app generates video files and, at the operator's request, uploads them to the operator's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page using those platforms' official APIs.

Data we access

Account access tokens for the operator's own YouTube/Instagram/ Facebook accounts, used solely to upload videos the operator created. Tokens are stored as environment variables/secrets on the operator's own server and are never shared. We also handle the video files and captions the operator creates.

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of any other person. We do not access followers, messages, comments, or audience data, and we do not scrape third-party content.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses the youtube.upload scope only to upload our own videos to our own channel; it does not transfer or use this data for any other purpose.

Data retention & deletion

Access tokens live only on the operator's server and can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings (Google Account permissions / Meta Business settings). Rendered videos are stored on the operator's server and may be deleted at any time. For deletion requests, contact the email below.
